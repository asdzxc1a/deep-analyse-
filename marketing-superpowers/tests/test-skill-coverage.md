# Skill Coverage Expectations

## Platform Reviewer Differences

- TikTok reviewer must care most about hook intensity, surprise, energy, and rewatch/share impulse.
- Instagram reviewer must care most about recognizability, creator identity, visual world, and slightly cleaner edit logic.
- YouTube reviewer must care most about story payoff, character depth, and longer retention.

## Core Skill Coverage

- `creator-diagnosis` must diagnose weak or random posting.
- `creator-content-planning` must produce a weekly content system.
- `contrast-message-architecture` must preserve the tomboy/high-fashion contrast.
- `viral-content-review` must judge hook strength and shareability.
- `posting-readiness-check` must verify publish readiness.
- `content-postmortem` must explain rewatches, shares, and what to repeat or stop.
