# Content Postmortem

Turn content performance into better next moves.

## Analyze

Always review:
- what held attention
- what drove rewatches
- what drove shares
- what should be repeated
- what should stop

## Output

Always produce:
- strongest performance signal
- weakest performance signal
- what to repeat
- what to stop
- what to test next

## Guardrail

Do not describe metrics without turning them into a next strategy decision.
