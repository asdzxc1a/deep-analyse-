# Viral Content Review

Review content for growth potential, not only aesthetics.

## Review Questions

Check:
- is the hook strong enough
- is the contrast visible fast enough
- is personality visible in the opening
- is the content likely to be shared or rewatched
- is the post strong enough to publish

## Required Review Output

Always produce:
- decision: publish / revise / reject
- strongest hook strength
- biggest weakness
- whether the contrast is clear
- whether the content feels viral or only pretty
