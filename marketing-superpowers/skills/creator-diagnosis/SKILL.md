# Creator Diagnosis

Diagnose the real growth problem before making more content.
This skill is specifically for weak or random posting.

## Purpose

Find:
- what is spreading
- what is only pretty
- what the real creator advantage is
- what content patterns should stop

## Focus Areas

1. Contrast strength
- Is the tomboy/high-fashion contrast obvious enough?

2. Hook quality
- Does the opening stop the scroll fast enough?

3. Personality visibility
- Does her funny, wild energy appear early enough?

4. Platform confusion
- Are TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube being used for the right jobs?

5. Posting randomness
- Is the account posting without a weekly content system?

## Output

Always produce:
- top growth blockers
- strongest creator edge
- content types to stop
- content types to double down on
- recommended next strategy move

## Guardrail

Do not jump to content ideas until the diagnosis explains why the current posting is weak or strong.
