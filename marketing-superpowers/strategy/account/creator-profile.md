# Creator Profile

## Core Identity

She is a cute rebel kid with chaotic charm and a Pixar-style troublemaker energy.

Her real edge is the contrast between:
- wild tomboy behavior
- funny, emotionally magnetic personality
- luxurious high-fashion transformation

## What Makes Her Watchable

- she feels alive immediately
- she is playful and rebellious
- the personality is the hook, not only the look
- the luxury payoff becomes more powerful because the baseline is chaotic

## Brand Statement

She is a cute rebel kid who turns into luxurious high-fashion looks without losing her wild personality.

## Non-Negotiables

- do not let the content become generic child fashion
- do not let pretty visuals replace character
- do not use lifestyle footage without a role in the creator world
- keep the rebel-to-luxury contrast visible
