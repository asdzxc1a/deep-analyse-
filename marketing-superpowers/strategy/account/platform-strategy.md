# Platform Strategy

## TikTok: virality engine

TikTok should lead with:
- immediate hook
- strongest surprise
- fastest contrast
- biggest personality in the first moments

Best content:
- rebel-to-luxury transformations
- cute chaos attitude clips
- repeatable fast series

## Instagram: identity engine

Instagram should lead with:
- strongest visual world
- recognizability
- polished but still high-energy edits
- repeated creator image

Best content:
- strongest transformation edits
- best covers
- repeatable series entries that define her image

## YouTube: attachment and authority engine

YouTube should lead with:
- story
- process
- personality depth
- transformation arc

Best content:
- behind-the-transformation story
- mini fashion narratives
- longer-form versions of the creator world
