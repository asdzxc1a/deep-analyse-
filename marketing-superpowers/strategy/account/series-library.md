# Series Library

## Core Series

### chaos to couture
- start with chaotic rebel energy
- end with a strong luxury transformation

### tiny rebel, big fashion energy
- show the mismatch between small size and big attitude
- pay off with expensive style

### she said no until the final look
- use refusal, attitude, or mini-conflict
- land on a surprising final reveal

### rebel girl becomes luxury icon
- stronger editorial version of the transformation format

## Attitude Series

### little boss energy
- short clips built on swagger, refusal, and playful control

### cute chaos check
- quick reaction or behavior clips that strengthen character recognition

## Behind-The-Scene Series

### from mood to look
- selection, prep, and fashion decision story

### before the reveal
- mini-story focused on tension before the final transformation
