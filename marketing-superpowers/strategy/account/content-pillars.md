# Content Pillars

## 1. Rebel-To-Luxury Transformations

Share:
- 50%

Purpose:
- main reach engine

Structure:
- start with rebel or chaotic baseline
- move quickly into the fashion shift
- land on a luxurious high-fashion payoff

## 2. Cute Chaos Attitude Moments

Share:
- 25%

Purpose:
- memorability
- attachment

Structure:
- reaction
- refusal
- swagger
- playful rebel energy

## 3. Main-Character Fashion Series

Share:
- 15%

Purpose:
- recurring audience behavior

Structure:
- recognizable format
- repeated title or theme
- consistent contrast payoff

## 4. Behind-The-Transformation Story

Share:
- 10%

Purpose:
- depth
- YouTube strength
- selected Instagram depth

Structure:
- prep
- choice
- tension
- reveal build-up

## Lifestyle And Travel Rule

Use lifestyle or travel only when it supports one of the four pillars above.
