# Fashion Creator Doctrine

## Core Thesis

This creator grows because the audience sees a contrast they want to watch again:
- wild, funny tomboy energy
- unexpected high-fashion transformation

The system must protect that contrast. If a strategy weakens it, the strategy is wrong.

## Non-Negotiables

- Research before messaging.
- Strategy before content batching.
- Review before posting.
- Verification before publish.
- Escalate when content conflicts with age, safety, or creator identity.
- Do not post polished content that has no growth reason.

## Growth Posture

The first goal is broad audience growth, virality, and recognizability.

The system should prefer:
- strong hooks
- fast contrast
- repeatable series
- posts that feel impossible to ignore

The system should avoid:
- generic fashion content
- luxury polish without personality
- content that looks nice but does not spread

## Platform Doctrine

- TikTok should chase reach, surprise, energy, and rewatch value.
- Instagram should strengthen recognizability and creator identity.
- YouTube should deepen attachment, story, and authority.

## Approval Gates

Approval is required:
- after diagnosis and before strategy
- after strategy and before batching
- after review and before publish

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to the human owner or creative director when:
- the identity becomes blurry
- the content loses the tomboy/high-fashion contrast
- the review loop repeats without improvement
- the concept creates brand-safety risk
- the content no longer fits the creator's age or boundaries
