# Mythic Couture Creative Reset Design

## Goal

Reset the fashion creator strategy pack around a stronger cold-audience thesis:

- she made couture feel alive again

The existing strategy pack established useful structure, but the first cold-audience execution layer stayed too procedural and too one-note. This slice promotes the deeper creative direction into the official strategy pack.

## Problem

The earlier concept and shooting boards over-relied on:
- rebel energy
- chaos
- attitude
- reveal mechanics

That created three problems:
- the character flattened into one emotional note
- the world lacked mythology and continuity
- the concepts felt strategically competent but not unforgettable

## New Creative Thesis

The outer creative language should be simple and mythic:
- she made couture feel alive again
- she woke up a dead fashion world
- she brought instinct into a space that expected control
- she entered an untouchable world and changed its mood

Underneath that outer hook, the inner character system should stay human:
- outwardly defiant
- inwardly fascinated by beauty
- protected by a quiet mother/stylist relationship
- discovering a fashion world that slowly becomes hers

## Official Pack Changes

This slice should make the new direction official in the checked strategy pack.

### 1. Strategy Spec

`marketing-superpowers/strategy/live-fashion-creator-strategy-spec.md` should describe:
- the master thesis
- the outer mythic hook and inner emotional layer
- the role of beauty, trust, fascination, and discovery

### 2. Concept Board

`marketing-superpowers/strategy/account/cold-audience-concept-board-v2-deep-analysis.md` should become an official checked artifact.

It should be treated as the active upgrade layer because it:
- critiques the first concept board honestly
- defines deeper psychology
- expands the character into multiple modes
- introduces recurring narrative and world-building logic

### 3. Legacy Execution Layer

The earlier production and scripted-board files should not be silently treated as the current creative north star.

They should stay available as a first production pass, but be labeled as needing rewrite against the mythic-couture thesis before filming the next cycle.

### 4. Validation

The strategy-pack checks should require the new concept board and verify key thesis phrases so the official strategy layer cannot drift back to the flatter frame.

## Success Criteria

This reset is successful when:
- the strategy pack clearly states the master thesis
- the new concept board is part of the validated pack
- the official creative language shifts from “rebel reveal” to “couture becomes alive”
- the old execution files are clearly marked as first-pass material, not the final creative standard
