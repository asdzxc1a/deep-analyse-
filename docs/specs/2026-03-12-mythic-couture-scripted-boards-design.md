# Mythic Couture Scripted Boards Design

## Goal

Rewrite the first production-grade execution documents so they match the official mythic-couture thesis:

- she made couture feel alive again

The existing creative reset made the stronger direction official in the strategy pack, but the actual shooting boards still reflected the older, flatter “rebel reveal” layer.

## Problem

The old scripted boards were structurally useful, but they carried the wrong creative center of gravity:
- too much emphasis on chaos and swagger
- not enough emphasis on fascination, world-building, and mythic statement
- not enough emotional range
- not enough continuity from post to post

This made the execution layer lag behind the official strategy.

## Design

The rewritten execution layer should work like this:

### 1. Production Board

`marketing-superpowers/strategy/account/production-board-weeks-1-2.md`

Purpose:
- map the first two weeks to the new episode-based arc
- turn the two weeks into an opening season instead of isolated posts
- organize filming by world/theme, not by generic platform buckets

### 2. Scripted Boards

`marketing-superpowers/strategy/account/scripted-shooting-boards-top-5.md`

Purpose:
- convert the strongest five episodes into real shoot documents
- make the character and psychology visible in the execution itself
- keep the same operational headings, but replace the old emotional logic

## Creative Rules

Each scripted board must:
- make the mythic claim legible fast
- show how couture becomes alive
- carry at least two audience reactions:
  - Recognition
  - Aspiration
  - Protective Tenderness
  - Surprise
  - Incompleteness

Each board should feel like an episode, not a transformation template.

## Selected Episodes

The first five boards should establish the opening world:
- Day 1: The Audition
- Day 2: She Noticed Something You Did Not
- Day 3: The Reject Pile
- Day 5: The Wrong Soundtrack
- Day 7: She Teaches You Something (And Gets It Slightly Wrong)

These cover:
- authority
- fascination
- recurring bit / world-building
- tenderness through mismatch
- quotable philosophy

## Validation

The strategy checks should enforce the new execution layer by requiring:
- episode names from the new arc
- the master thesis phrase in the scripted boards
- deeper psychology markers such as `Protective Tenderness`

## Success Criteria

This slice is successful when:
- the production board schedules the mythic-couture episodes directly
- the scripted boards no longer read like legacy rebel-reveal concepts
- the strategy checks pass against the rewritten execution files
